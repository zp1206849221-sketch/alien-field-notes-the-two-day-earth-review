# Alien Field Notes: The Two Day Earth Review

A one-line entry for the make.fun contest "Alien Field Notes: The Two Day
Earth Review". An alien field researcher files a two-day review of Earth and
completely misreads the planet's power structure: after watching humans trail
behind their dogs and reverently bag what the dogs leave behind, the visitor
concludes the small four-legged creatures are the rulers and the tall ones
are staff. The line carries a tourist-style 4/5 star rating up front and lets
the "sacred relics" punch land at the very end.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the entry (a single line of plain text, at most 200 characters)
to `/output/entry.txt`, creating the output directory if needed.
